Common questions

If your question isn't here, send it through the contact form below.

What exactly does rights management cover?
It covers how your content is claimed, licensed, and monetized on YouTube — from setting up ownership correctly to resolving disputes when something is claimed incorrectly.
How long does a copyright claim usually take to resolve?
It depends on the type of claim and the claimant, but most straightforward disputes move within a couple of weeks once we have full details of your case.
Do you work with channels outside India?
Yes. Rights and copyright issues on YouTube aren't tied to a single country, and we work with creators wherever they're based.
Is there a minimum channel size to get started?
No. We work with channels at any stage, from just getting started to established, monetized channels.
